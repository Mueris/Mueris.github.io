Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

This policy explains which personal data the Bul Bakalım mobile game (iOS and Android), developed by Musseray Games, processes and why, who we share it with and how you can exercise your rights. If you have any questions or requests, write to us at Bul_Bakalim@outlook.com.tr.

Data we collect

Account information. If you play as a guest we do not ask for an email address and your account gets a random user ID. If you register with email and password we process your email address, and if you sign in with Apple we process the account identifier and email address Apple shares with us. If you chose to hide your email, this is the relay address provided by Apple. Sign-in runs through Google Firebase Authentication, and we never see or store your password.

Game data. Your username, level and experience points, gold and gem balance, owned avatars and items, joker and skill settings, mission and daily reward progress, match statistics, language preference and the bug notes you write inside the game are stored with your account in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. To keep usernames unique, the name you choose is also kept in a list of usernames.

Match data. During online matches your username, avatar, level and in-match moves (questions, answers, guesses and joker uses) are sent to our game server and shared with your opponent. Our game server runs on Render and technically sees your IP address in order to connect. We do not use match data for anything other than running the game.

Preferences on your device. Sound, music and similar settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

Advertising data. Data collected by our advertising partner Google AdMob is described in the Advertising section below.

Bul Bakalım does not use a separate analytics or crash reporting tool. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Advertising

Bul Bakalım is free and supported by Google AdMob ads. The game shows full-screen interstitial ads between matches and rewarded ads that you watch only if you choose to. To serve and measure ads, limit their frequency and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Google Advertising ID on Android), your IP address, device and app information and your interactions with ads. This data is subject to Google's own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form for advertising data, and personalized ads are shown only with consent. In these regions you can change your consent at any time with Privacy Options in the game's Settings window.

On iOS, your permission to use the advertising identifier is requested through Apple's tracking prompt. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are shown without personalization. The whole game can be played without giving permission. You can also use your device's privacy settings to reset your advertising identifier or turn off personalized ads.

Why we process data

We process your data to create your account and save your game progress, run online matches and match you with opponents, keep usernames unique, prevent cheating and abuse, find and fix bugs, answer support requests and keep the game free by showing ads. The legal bases for this processing are that it is necessary to provide the game to you, our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe and fair, and, for advertising data, your consent.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is shared only with service providers that help us run the game, and only as far as these services require. These providers are Google (Firebase and AdMob) for authentication, database and advertising services, Apple for Sign in with Apple and Render for hosting the game server. During a match your opponents see your username, avatar, level and match moves. If a legal obligation arises, we may share data with competent authorities.

The servers of our service providers may be located outside Türkiye, for example in the European Union or the United States. In that case your data is processed abroad under the security and data protection commitments of these providers.

How long we keep data

Your account and game data are kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account in the app, your account, username and game progress are permanently deleted right away. Deletion requests sent by email are completed within 30 days at the latest. For guest accounts, sign-in is tied to the session on your device, and removing the app from your device removes access to that session but does not delete the record on our servers. To delete that record too, use the Delete My Account option before removing the app, or write to us.

Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and data right inside the app. Open your profile, go to Settings and tap Delete My Account, and the deletion happens immediately. If you cannot access the app, send an email with the subject "Account deletion request" to Bul_Bakalim@outlook.com.tr and include your in-game username and, if registered, the email address of your account. Details are on the Account deletion page.

Children

Bul Bakalım is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13, and if we learn that we have, we delete that data. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us data, please write to us.

Security

Traffic between the app, Firebase and our game server travels over encrypted connections (TLS). Data is accessed only as far as needed to operate the game. No system is completely risk free, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 and, in Europe, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to access it, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its processing, to receive it in a portable format and to withdraw any consent you have given. To exercise these rights, simply write to Bul_Bakalim@outlook.com.tr, and we will respond within 30 days at the latest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The current version is always on this page, and the date of the last update is shown at the top.
